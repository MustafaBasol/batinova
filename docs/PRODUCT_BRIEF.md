# Product brief — BatiNova Construction

## Confirmed by owner
- Brand: BatiNova Construction; slogan: Construire autrement.
- Website: btnv.fr (target canonical: https://btnv.fr).
- Base address supplied: 4 rue des Lilas, 93600 Aulnay-sous-Bois, France.
- Regions served: Île-de-France; Normandie, with Manche highlighted. Manche is a department in Normandie.
- Logo source: `assets/batinova-logo-reference.jpeg`.
- French-only public site; premium minimalist architecture style; SEO critical; blog and self-service owner CMS mandatory.

## Navigation
Accueil; À propos; Nos services; Nos réalisations; Notre méthode; Zones d’intervention; Blog / Conseils; Contact / Demander un devis; Mentions légales; Politique de confidentialité; Gestion des cookies only if needed.

## Functional scope (v1)
Public: responsive content pages, contact/quote form, service region content, real portfolio when available, blog index/detail, legal pages, media optimization and SEO. Admin: owner/editor auth, dashboard, block-based CMS pages, service pages, blog with categories/tags/drafts/schedules, portfolios, library and ALT/licensing, configurable site-wide contacts, lead inbox and export, redirects and SEO controls, audit trail.

## Suggested mail identities (unprovisioned)
contact@btnv.fr (general), devis@btnv.fr (leads), projets@btnv.fr (project coordination), direction@btnv.fr (management), privacy@btnv.fr (data requests). Use configurable addresses; do not assert mailboxes or DNS exist. Prefer two real inboxes and aliases as needed.

## Open owner verification items (DO NOT invent)
Exact legal entity name and status, SIREN/SIRET/RCS/TVA, phone and contact name, validated address and actual operating sectors, permitted service list, scope of insurance/assurance décennale and its certificate, verified project images/customer permissions, project references, domain/DNS/mail hosting access, privacy controller details, hosting identity, published marketing claims. The initial UI may use prominently labeled test content but must not publish claims as facts.

## Design acceptance
Distinctive high-end aesthetic using logo palette, restrained motion, compelling full-width architecture imagery with correct licensing, trust through real evidence, obvious quote CTA, clean mobile navigation, fast LCP, clear readable content and accessible controls.

## Release policy
Only local / isolated staging changes without secrets. No PR/merge/deploy/DNS changes without a later explicit user command. CodeGraph targeted source-path exploration only.
